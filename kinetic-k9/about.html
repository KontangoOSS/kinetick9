Our Story

Kinetic K9 was founded on a simple but powerful belief: every dog is capable of greatness, and every owner deserves the confidence to bring it out. What started as a single trainer working with neighborhood dogs has grown into a full-service professional training center serving hundreds of families each year.

Our founder, a lifelong dog enthusiast and certified professional trainer, built Kinetic K9 after witnessing firsthand how outdated, force-based training methods were damaging the relationships between dogs and their owners. Driven by a commitment to evidence-based, compassionate training, Kinetic K9 was born.

Today, our growing team of certified trainers brings expertise in behavioral science, canine psychology, and practical real-world training to every session. We serve clients across the greater Palo Alto area with in-home sessions, group classes, and online resources — all with the same philosophy at the core: respect, patience, and positive reinforcement.

Our Training Philosophy

At Kinetic K9, we follow a positive reinforcement, least-intrusive, minimally aversive (LIMA) approach to dog training. This means we always look for the most humane and effective solution before moving to more intrusive techniques.

We believe in teaching dogs what we want them to do, rather than punishing them for what we don't. By rewarding desired behaviors generously and setting dogs up to succeed, we create dogs who are eager, confident, and genuinely enjoy working with their owners.

Our methods are endorsed by the Association of Professional Dog Trainers (APDT), the Certification Council for Professional Dog Trainers (CCPDT), and aligned with the American Veterinary Society of Animal Behavior (AVSAB) guidelines.
