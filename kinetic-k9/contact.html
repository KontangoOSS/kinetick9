Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How long does training typically take? ▾

It depends on the dog, the behavior, and your consistency at home. Most clients see significant improvement within 4–6 sessions for basic obedience. Behavior modification programs for reactivity or anxiety typically take longer — often 8–12 weeks of consistent work.

Do you use punishment-based methods? ▾

Absolutely not. Kinetic K9 uses exclusively positive reinforcement and force-free methods grounded in modern behavioral science. We don't use prong collars, shock collars, choke chains, or any form of physical correction. Our approach is kind, effective, and lasting.

What age can I start training my puppy? ▾

The sooner, the better! We recommend starting training from the day you bring your puppy home — even at 8 weeks. The socialization window closes around 12–16 weeks, making early enrollment in our Puppy Foundation program one of the best investments you can make.

Do you offer virtual or online training sessions? ▾

No, to best be able to help you and your dog, in person training is the best key for success!

Is the free consultation really free? ▾

Yes, 100% free with no obligation. We offer a free 30-minute in-person consultation to every new client. It's our way of getting to know you and your dog, answering your questions honestly, and making sure we're the right fit before you commit to anything.
