Why Choose Kinetic K9?

At Kinetic K9, we believe every dog has incredible potential. Our experienced trainers use the latest behavioral science to create lasting, positive change — for both dogs and their owners.

We don't just train dogs — we educate owners. Our goal is to give you the tools and confidence to maintain a strong, respectful bond with your dog long after the training sessions end.

  • Personalized training plans tailored to your dog
  • Positive reinforcement & reward-based methods
  • Puppy socialization & early development programs
  • Behavior modification for reactive & anxious dogs
  • Ongoing support and follow-up sessions

Our Training Programs

Tailored solutions for every dog, at every stage of life.

🐶

Puppy Foundation

Build the perfect foundation for your puppy with socialization, bite inhibition, basic commands, and house manners. Best for pups 8–16 weeks old.

🎓

Basic Obedience

Master the essential commands — sit, stay, come, heel, and more. Perfect for dogs of any age who need structure and reliable responses.

🏆

Advanced Off-Leash

Take your dog's training to the next level with off-leash reliability, distance commands, and distractions in real-world environments.

😰

Behavior Modification

Specialized programs for reactivity, aggression, fear, and anxiety. We use proven desensitization and counter-conditioning techniques.

🏠

In-Home Training

We bring expert training directly to your home — the place where most behavior issues occur. Convenient, personalized, and highly effective.

👥

Group Classes

Affordable group classes that teach your dog to perform around distractions while building social skills. Great for all experience levels.
